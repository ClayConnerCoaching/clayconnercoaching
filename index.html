About the work

The leader you already are.

I've spent most of my adult life in rooms with people who lead — and I kept noticing the same thing. Not their accomplishments. The distance between the leader they were and the leader they showed up as when it counted. I do this work to close that distance, with them, from the inside.

I'm not a consultant. I won't hand you a plan and call it leadership. I sit with you in the hard questions — honest ones, about who you are — until what you find changes how you lead.

I came to formal training after years of doing this work. The structure added precision to what was already there. The practice came first, and that order shows up in how it runs.

I work with a select number of leaders at a time. I keep it that way on purpose. This work needs attention, not a system that scales.
