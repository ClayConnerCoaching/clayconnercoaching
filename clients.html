01
Dr. Andrew Rostenberg
Chiropractor · Boise, Idaho
He knew exactly who he was. He couldn't lead from it when it counted.

A chiropractor who knew exactly who he was but couldn't lead from it when the care-plan conversation turned to money. Over a thirteen-year relationship that began at his lowest point, the work changed how he showed up — and the practice followed.

Documented results
  • Reframed the fee conversation from a negotiation into an act of service — patients responded
  • Six patients a week to fifty-five; past $24,000 a month within six months
  • Built the full patient-journey system and opened his own practice
  • Moved from technician to builder — hired and developed a team, designed staff retention
  • Thirteen years later: leads a multi-physician clinic and trains other doctors
“I've tried to do so many things by myself. Having someone listen — and then ask the question that gets things to move — that's what changed everything.”Dr. Andrew Rostenberg
02
Savannah Rossetti
Principal Consultant
Leading herself when the room moved without her.

A high performer rebuilding after a loss who learned to read her own values as a guide. Months later she carried that same precision into a senior role that had been redrawn without her in the room.

Documented results
  • Built a portable decision tool she still uses — moves without over-deliberating
  • Set her criteria before entering a new role; walked in at full confidence
  • Defined and held her role under pressure when it was redrawn without her
  • Found the language for a hard conversation with leadership and held her ground
  • Set hard boundaries to protect her health and capacity — chose herself over overwork
“I'm not willing to settle for mediocre in work any more than I would in relationships. If it's not a hell yes, it's a no.”Savannah Rossetti
03
Luke Larkin
AI Product Builder · Boise, Idaho
Leading a team he couldn't be honest with.

A founder with a clear vision and no one he could be fully honest with — least of all the team that needed him certain. The work surfaced the belief running beneath his leadership.

Documented results
  • Named the pattern beneath his leadership — once named, it lost its grip
  • Anchored his leadership on his own phrase: clarity of purpose
  • Worked the shift from caregiver to a leader who sets standards and owns his space
  • Changed how he leads his team — by choice, not instruction
“I couldn't unsee what he showed me about myself.”Luke Larkin
04
Melanie Thorley
Employment & Labor Law · Brisbane, Australia (flight training)
Proven in one arena. Starting over in another — with everything on the line.

A courtroom advocate who froze in an unfamiliar domain where her competence didn't transfer. After 100 hours of flight training stalled and ready to quit, the work was carrying the authority she already had into the place she didn't have it yet.

Documented results
  • Named the inner voice running every final approach — and stopped following it
  • Found “clarity of action” — understood her own thinking and could redirect it
  • Carried proven professional authority into unfamiliar, high-stakes terrain
  • Passed her checkride on the first attempt, weeks after nearly giving up
“Clarity of action. I was able to understand why I was doing something in my own mind — and coaching gave me the clarity to shift that into a different path.”Melanie Thorley
